Phase 00

The Origin

A simple rotation, an infinite vision

It began with one question: what if a wheelchair could be more than metal and wheels? What if every rotation could become intelligence that protects and empowers its user?

From that single spark, the WheelSense team formed around one conviction: assistive technology deserves the same ambition and engineering depth as any frontier innovation.

Phase 01 - Digitization

YES Wheelchair

Physical to Digital

We did not see a wheelchair as only equipment. We saw a continuous data source. YES Wheelchair embedded IoT sensors to convert movement into measurable science: distance, calories, and fall-detection signals.

For the first time, the wheelchair could speak in data, and caregivers could act in time.

Phase 02 - Interaction

ALL Wheelchair

Digital to Interaction

Data alone is not enough; motivation is the missing layer. ALL Wheelchair transformed movement into game control, combining rehabilitation with exergaming so exercise feels rewarding and sustainable.

Therapy shifted from repetitive routines into adaptive, engaging interaction.

Phase 03 - Expansion

Marathon and Racing

Room to World

We extended the core system beyond indoor spaces with GPS and 4G modules. Telemetry now follows wheelchair users across full race courses with live coordinates, speed, and endurance analytics.

Real-world movement at scale became observable, trackable, and coachable.

Phase 04 - Intelligence

WheelSense

Vehicle to Companion

The newest era fuses AI, computer vision, and smart-home control. WheelSense is designed to understand context, detect situations, and help users interact with their environment more independently.

Assistive technology is no longer passive transport. It becomes an intelligent companion.
