Phase 0 — Origin

Phase 00

The Origin

A simple rotation, an infinite vision

It began with a question: what if a wheelchair could be more than metal and wheels? What if every rotation could tell a story — of distance traveled, of challenges overcome, of lives transformed?

From that single spark of imagination, Omniwheel was born. Not as a company, but as a conviction that technology should serve the overlooked and underserved with the same precision and passion as any breakthrough product.

Phase 01 — Digitization

Yes WheelChair

Physical → Digital

We didn't see the wheelchair as merely equipment. We saw it as a source of data. Yes WheelChair was our first step — embedding IoT sensors to transform every movement into measurable science: distance, calories, and a fall detection system that watches over safety.

For the first time, the wheelchair could speak — in the language of data.

Phase 02 — Interaction

All Wheelchair

Digital → Interaction

Data alone isn't enough. Inspiration is what matters. All Wheelchair transformed the wheelchair into a game controller — merging physical therapy with exergaming so users exercise through joy, tearing down the walls of monotonous training.

Rehabilitation became an experience, not an obligation.

Phase 03 — Expansion

Marathon & Racing

Room → World

From indoor rooms to worldwide racetracks. We gave the heart of Yes WheelChair wings with GPS and 4G modules. Distance barriers shattered. Real-time telemetry for wheelchair marathon competitions — tracking position and speed with precision, no matter where on the course.

Phase 04 — Intelligence

WheelSense

Vehicle → Companion

The ultimate evolution: a wheelchair that thinks. WheelSense fuses AI and computer vision with smart home ecosystems. It doesn't just carry you — it understands your context, controls your home by voice, and watches over you like a personal assistant.

This is the new definition of assistive technology.
