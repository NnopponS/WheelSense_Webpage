The Future of Assistive Technology

WheelSense is a technology collective born from the belief that wheelchairs can be more than mobility devices - they can be intelligent companions. We are engineers, designers, and innovators who refuse to accept limitations.

Over four eras of innovation, we've evolved from embedding simple sensors into wheelchairs to creating AI-driven ecosystems that understand, protect, and empower their users. Every breakthrough is guided by a single principle: technology should serve humanity with precision and compassion.

Our Journey

Four Eras of Innovation

Phase 01 - Digitization

YES Wheelchair

Transforming physical motion into measurable data. IoT sensors embedded to track distance, calories, and safety through fall detection.

Phase 02 - Interaction

ALL Wheelchair

Turning rehabilitation into engagement. Motion-controlled gaming that makes physical therapy an experience, not a chore.

Phase 03 - Expansion

smartVibe

Expanding into outdoor intelligent mobility with connected telemetry, route context, and adaptive interaction.

Phase 04 - Intelligence

WheelSense

The ultimate evolution - AI and computer vision integrated with smart home ecosystems. A wheelchair that understands context.
